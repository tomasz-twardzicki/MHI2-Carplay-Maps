match test1. `wireless-entry` is listening on loopback port 9997. The receiver

## Missed dependency

Test1 deliberately omitted upstream `altscreen.conf` and `altscreen.geom` to
preserve existing wired settings. The exact receiver's alternate-display gate
at ARM VA `0xe4330` actually requires a readable probe flag, or a readable
`/mnt/persist/altscreen.conf` with no readable `/mnt/persist/wcp_alt_off`.
Exporting `WCP_ALT_W/H` and the feed port does not satisfy this file-based gate.
The first collector did not capture these config/off/probe paths, so their
individual on-unit existence still needs confirmation. The non-advertisement
and missing feed are directly observed; omission of the config is the matching
test1 integration defect, with the existing off flag another possible gate input.

[`RECEIVER-ALT-GATE.json`](RECEIVER-ALT-GATE.json) records eight passing checks
executing that actual function in the unchanged pinned receiver under QNX.
A test-only preload calls it before receiver main and exits without opening
hardware/network services. This is not a phone or AID playback test.

## Controlled vehicle probe

The rebuilt GEM page adds **Enable AID display probe (until reboot)**. The
action checks the installed layer, MU1367/791 profile and exact receiver hash;
it refuses an explicit wireless/alternate-display off flag or a symlink target.
It writes only `/dev/shmem/wcp_alt_probe`. It does not alter persistent files,
installed runtime payloads or snapshots. A full reboot clears the probe.

Refresh menus/scripts from the new SD, run that action, then disconnect and
reconnect wireless CarPlay without rebooting and with USB unplugged. Open
navigation and collect diagnostics again. Expected evidence is an alternate
display in `/info`, a connection/feed at port 9997, matched accepted/feed
generations and AID supervisor takeover. The updated collector includes the
missing config/off/probe state. A successful vehicle probe is required before
choosing and packaging a permanent activation policy.
