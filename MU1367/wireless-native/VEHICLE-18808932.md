## Missing evidence

The inherited exporter copies `carplay*`, `altscreen*`, `cpmap*` and `pmcp-aa*`
from `/ramdisk`, but omits the wireless logs in `/dev/shmem`, the
`omonob-791-input` log/PID, `v75-wireless-native.owner`, and scoped acceptance /
feed states. Its layer summary also omits `carplay-mu1367-wireless-native`.
The process listing omits wireless process names and does not reliably select
MMX when invoked from RCC.

Consequently this bundle cannot distinguish a missing/failed input listener,
an absent alternate video stream, an unaccepted session or a rejected ownership
contract. Do not relax the ownership checks or change the bridge on that basis.
