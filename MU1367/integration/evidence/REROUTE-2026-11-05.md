# Reroute capture analysis, 2026-11-05

Inputs: the private MU1427-era guidance journals in the user's `Logs` folder (C4, D1,
D2 and D3). Raw captures stay outside Git. No journal records which app was active.
These test routes are short, so distances are small.

## What a reroute looks like

All 16 reroutes in the journals follow one sequence:

| Step | Route state | Timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guiding | 1 | |
| Rerouting | 5 | |
| Stop | 0, usually repeated | At most 322 ms after state 5 |
| Loading | 3 | 0–110 ms later, in the reroute cases |
| Guiding | 1 | At most 113 ms later |

Maneuver records may arrive around these states and are re-sent after state 3. The new
route's list then starts at index 0, a start-route maneuver (iAP2 type 11) with a
maneuver distance of 0. In the sampled journal this lasted about 5 to 15 s, then the
list advanced and real distances resumed. Indexes are reused by the new route.

## Why the countdown restarted

The model cleared its route state at states 5 and 0, then treated state 1 as a new
trip. On a replay of one journal through v1.4.4:

| Time (ms) | v1.4.4 | v1.4.5-e |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 56104 | Guiding, 30 m | Guiding, 30 m |
| 57065 | Off, distance lost | Calculating, 30 m |
| 57388 | Placeholder, 0 m | Calculating, 30 m |
| 62193 | Guiding, 40 m | Guiding, 40 m |

## Result on all journals

Replaying the journals through the new model gave 12 distinct reroute episodes, with
guidance active throughout. The calculating symbol lasted from about 0.1 to 25 s. The
longest cases come from the start-route step at 0 m rather than the phone's calculation,
and the bound is only checked when a message arrives.

## Not established

- Vehicle rendering, including whether the AID draws the progress bar on `CALC_ROUTE`.
  The symbol sweep did not try that combination.
- App behavior. A phone that goes from state 1 to 0 without state 5 or 3 is treated as a
  normal stop, so the counters would still reset. Apple Maps has not been captured in a
  reroute.
